# Curriculum Vitae

## TL;DR

I am a software engineer with strengths in test automation, quality assurance, DevOps, and Scrum. I have a strong interest in process improvement and connecting processes to business objectives, and I can contribute to solving issues such as:

- There is a barrier between development and QA, resulting in a "I make it, you test it" mentality.
- Although practicing Agile development, frequent incidents occur, or there is a fear of release due to the risk of incidents.
- Initiatives like test automation are desired, but it is difficult to measure ROI, making company-wide investment a challenge.

I also have extensive experience as a Software Engineer in Test (SET), specializing in building and operating test automation infrastructure and troubleshooting. I am particularly experienced in the area of E2E (end-to-end) testing—system testing and acceptance testing—and knowledgeable in practices such as BDD/ATDD.

With my holistic understanding of entire software business, including product design, development, marketing, sales, and support, I can contribute to the total optimization of business and product.

## Career

### Overview

After five years of experience in logistics management in the office supply distribution industry, I worked as a web application developer at an IT company providing pharmaceutical supply chain management (SCM) systems. I then joined the logistics startup OPENLOGI as their first QA engineer. Utilizing my background as a software developer and my domain knowledge of logistics management, I built and established the company’s quality assurance processes and test automation infrastructure.

Since 2019, I have been with Autify, a company offering an AI-powered test automation platform, where I helped launch the technical support team and served as a QA manager, supporting development teams in various capacities. Currently, as a Quality Evangelist at Autify, my mission is to promote the importance of investing in quality throughout society.

I am the author of the book "[Practical Guide to Test Automation](https://www.shoeisha.co.jp/book/detail/9784798172354)" and have delivered numerous talks on test automation and quality topics. I also actively contribute to the community as an organizing committee member for the online software testing symposium "[JaSST Online](https://jasst.jp/online/)" and the annual global conference "[Tokyo Test Fest](https://www.tokyotestfest.jp)" held in Tokyo.
